Contact

Email works best. A person reads it.

matt@panelready.co, any time. Replies usually come the same business day, from the person who runs PanelReady.

Useful things to put in the email

  • Free lists: the quickest way is the form, but an email with your company, state and category works just as well.
  • Changing your list: subscribers can reply to any PanelReady email with the new state or category ("switch to dental", "add New Jersey") and the next list reflects it.
  • Several states or an exclusive arrangement: say which states and categories and we'll quote a bundle.
  • Your account page: subscribers can open it from Account at the top of any page: enter your email and we send a sign-in link within the hour.
  • Cancelling: from your PayPal account under Subscriptions (your lists continue to the end of the period you've paid for), or reply STOP to any email (our emails stop right away and we cancel the PayPal subscription for you). No forms.
  • Billing questions: charges appear as Avery Data Co., PanelReady's parent company. Include the PayPal subscription ID (starts with I-) if you have it.

Mailing address

Avery Data Co., New York. The full postal address is in the footer of every email we send, as the law requires.
