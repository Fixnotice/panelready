The service

PanelReady sends you a spreadsheet of healthcare practices and sole proprietors that registered for an NPI in the previous week, for the state(s) and category(ies) on your plan, on the morning CMS posts the weekly file (normally once a week; CMS occasionally skips or delays a week). Your first list goes out as soon as you sign up and we know your territory, usually within minutes, from the latest weekly file (if that file has nothing for your territory, with the next one). A subscription's first paid list goes out as soon as your payment comes through and we know your territory, usually within minutes, and also includes every new practice in your territory from the previous 90 days; after that, lists follow the weekly schedule above. The data comes from the public NPPES registry exactly as published. We sort it, score each practice and suggest an opener; for organizations we also look up websites, emails, Google ratings and the practice software their websites use, from public sources (Google and the practice's own website), and we check every name against the federal OIG exclusion list. Each week subscribers also get the practices whose registered location changed in the registry that week, and once a month two more lists: practices due (or overdue) for Medicare revalidation according to CMS's public Revalidation Due Date List, and practices that registered 2-12 months earlier and don't appear on Medicare's public enrollment list. A "Medicare pending" flag means the provider appears on CMS's public list of pending first-time applications. Scores, openers, practice-size counts and lookups are our best suggestions, not verified facts; dates and statuses are as CMS publishes them.

The 90-day guarantee

If you don't sign at least one new client from your PanelReady lists during your first 90 days as a paying subscriber, tell us by email within 30 days after that and your next three months are free: we pause your PayPal subscription for three months, then it resumes at your normal price. One guarantee per company; no proof required.

Free lists

New companies can receive two weekly lists free, no payment details required. Free lists contain the weekly spreadsheet (with website and email lookups for its top 25 practices), the practices that moved or changed that week, a CRM import file and the outreach kit; the 90-day history, the monthly lists, the account page and Search & export come with a subscription. After the second list, unless you subscribe, you receive a short free weekly digest (the best new practices by name, without contact details) until you reply STOP. We may decline or limit free lists at our discretion, for example to one per company.
