Where the numbers come from

New practices come from the federal NPPES registry's weekly files (organizations and sole proprietors; employees and students who get an NPI only to work somewhere else are left out). Moved is a practice whose registered address changed to a new location. Stuck in credentialing is a practice that registered 2 to 12 months ago in a specialty that normally bills Medicare and still isn't on Medicare's public enrollment list. Revalidation due is Medicare's own list of enrollments due from 90 days ago through the next six months. Counts are rebuilt every week; the two monthly lists, every month.

This page shows counts only. The lists themselves come with the practice phone, the owner's name and own phone, the address, the specialty, a score and an opener for every row: two weekly lists are free.
