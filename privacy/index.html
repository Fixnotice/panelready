Privacy policy

Short, because we collect very little.

This policy covers panelready.co and the PanelReady email service, operated by Avery Data Co. ("we"). Last updated October 18, 2026.

What we collect

  • When you ask for lists or subscribe: your email address, company name, the state and category you chose, and, for paid plans, the PayPal subscription ID. PayPal handles the payment itself; we never see your card or bank details.
  • When you email us: the email and anything you put in it.
  • When you visit the site: nothing we keep. The site has no analytics, no advertising pixels and sets no cookies of its own. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose servers keep standard access logs (IP address, pages requested) under GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when it serves them.

What we do with it

We use your details to send you the lists you asked for, confirmations, and replies to your messages. We don't sell, rent or share them with anyone except the services that make the product work: PayPal for payments, our email provider for sending, and Cloudflare, which receives form submissions before we collect them. We don't use your data for advertising.

Email

Every email we send carries our postal address and a way to stop. Reply STOP to any message and you'll receive nothing further; the address goes on a do-not-contact list we keep for that purpose. We also send a small number of introductory emails to credentialing and billing companies found through public sources; the same STOP applies.

The data in the lists

The practices in our lists are organizations and sole proprietors who registered with CMS, taken from the public NPPES file as published. If you are a provider and want your public record changed, that is done with CMS through NPPES; we republish the file as it stands. If you'd rather not appear on this website's sample pages, email us and we'll remove the row from the pages we control.

Keeping and deleting it

We keep subscriber details while you're a subscriber and for our accounting afterwards. Email matt@panelready.co to see what we hold about you or to have it deleted; we'll do that within 30 days, keeping only what the law requires us to keep (for example payment records).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions: matt@panelready.co.
