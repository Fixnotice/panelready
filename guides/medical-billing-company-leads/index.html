How medical billing companies find new practices before they pick a biller

By Matt, PanelReady's founder · Numbers from the week ending November 8, 2026

Short answer: A new practice settles how it will handle billing in its first weeks, often before it sees a patient, and the earliest public sign that it exists is its NPI registration. CMS publishes every new registration in a free weekly file: filter it to organizations and sole proprietors in your state and specialties, and call in the first month. The week ending November 8, 2026 had 4,952 new practices nationwide, 1,906 of them in behavioral health.

Why the NPI registration comes first

A practice can't bill any insurer without an NPI, so it registers before it enrolls with payers, before its website is live and usually before it shows up on Google. That makes CMS's weekly NPPES file the earliest list of practices that will need billing set up. By the time a practice has reviews and a finished website, it has often already decided who does its billing.

Which new practices to call first

  • Solo and small practices. A sole proprietor or a two-provider group often has nobody on staff to do billing: the natural buyer of outsourced billing.
  • Organizations with an authorized official listed. The registry names the person who signed the NPI application, usually the owner or administrator, with a phone number.
  • Your specialties. Behavioral health, therapy, home health and DME each bill under their own rules, so most billing companies do best in a few. Filter to the ones you already know.
  • Early. The first month after registering is when the decision gets made.

Established practices are worth a call too

New practices aren't the only ones changing how they bill. Every weekly file also shows practices that moved, and a move means a new address to update with every payer. Each month CMS's lists also show practices due for Medicare revalidation (53,257 on the November 2026 list) and new practices still missing from Medicare's enrollment list months after registering (20,640), which means they can't bill Medicare yet.

Questions

How do medical billing companies find new clients?

Mostly through referrals, partnerships and outreach. For outreach, the most timely list is newly registered practices from CMS's weekly NPPES file, because a new practice decides how to handle billing in its first weeks.
