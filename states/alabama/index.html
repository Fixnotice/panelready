Questions about the Alabama list

How many new healthcare practices open in Alabama each month?

About 156 a month at the current pace (36 in the week ending October 11, 2026; 36 a week on average over the weeks tracked). The count includes new organizations and individuals who registered as sole proprietors; employees who registered for an NPI to take a job are excluded.

Where does this data come from?

The National Plan and Provider Enumeration System (NPPES), run by CMS. Every provider and organization that bills insurance needs an NPI, and CMS publishes the weekly registrations as a public file. PanelReady reads that file, keeps the practices and sole proprietors in Alabama, sorts them into six categories and refreshes this page every week.

How fresh are the names on this page?

CMS posts each week's file about a week after the week ends, and most registrations in it are one to six days old at that point, so a practice shows up here one to two weeks after it registered. Subscribers get the full list the morning the file is posted.

What does a subscriber get that this page doesn't show?

Every Alabama row, not a sample, with the complete phone number, street address, ZIP, NPI, taxonomy code, credential and authorized official, as a spreadsheet every Monday. PanelReady does not sell email addresses; the registry doesn't contain them.
