This category covers counselors, social workers, psychologists, psychiatry and psychiatric NPs, ABA providers, residential treatment, substance-use programs and peer specialists. The most common specialties this week were Behavior technician (71), Mental health counselor (25), Behavior analyst (20). At this pace Florida adds about 802 new behavioral health practices a month, each of which will need payer credentialing and a billing setup before it can collect on a claim.

That is 0.8 new behavioral health practices per 100,000 Florida residents this week, a smaller share of the state's registrations (34%) than this category takes nationally (38%). Next door, Georgia had 48, Alabama had 19. Each of these practices has to enroll with Florida Medicaid and the commercial plans it wants to bill before it can collect, which is the stretch of weeks in which it chooses a credentialing or billing partner.
