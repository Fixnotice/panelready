19 new behavioral health practices registered in Arkansas last week

19 behavioral health practices and sole proprietors registered for an NPI in Arkansas in the week ending October 11, 2026, 49% of the state's 39 new registrations and 1.0% of the 1,943 behavioral health registrations nationwide.

This category covers counselors, social workers, psychologists, psychiatry and psychiatric NPs, ABA providers and substance-use programs. The most common specialties this week were Mental health counselor (7), Behavior technician (6), Clinical social worker (2). At this pace Arkansas adds about 82 new behavioral health practices a month, each of which will need payer credentialing and a billing setup before it can collect on a claim.

That is 0.6 new behavioral health practices per 100,000 Arkansas residents this week, a bigger share of the state's registrations (49%) than this category takes nationally (40%). Next door, Texas had 184, Tennessee had 28, Oklahoma had 26. Each of these practices has to enroll with Arkansas Medicaid and the commercial plans it wants to bill before it can collect, which is the stretch of weeks in which it chooses a credentialing or billing partner.
